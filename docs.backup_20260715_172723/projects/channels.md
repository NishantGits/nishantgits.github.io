FlowChat's Channels provide organized, persistent workspaces for high-performance team collaboration.

## What Are Channels?

Channels are dedicated spaces where you can:
- **Group Conversations**: Organize work by project, department, or topic.
- **Collaborate**: Shared spaces for team-wide AI interactions and collective prompting.
- **Persistence**: Maintain full conversation history and search across discussions.

## Features

### Team Workspaces
**Private & Public**: Control access with invitation-only or team-wide channels. **Topics & Archiving**: Categorize conversations by theme and archive old channels to keep the workspace clean. **Pinned Messages**: Highlight important prompts or AI responses for easy access.

### Flexible Access Control
**Permissions**: Manage who can view or participate in specific channels. **Organization-Wide Settings**: Set global default models and system prompts for the workspace. **Team Management**: Securely manage participants and access within your organization.

### Knowledge Sharing
**Shared Context**: Team members can iterate on prompts together, share successful AI interactions, and build a collective knowledge base. **Shared Notes**: Collaborate on Markdown documents and link them to your AI chats. **Scoped Search**: Find specific messages instantly within a channel's context or search across all shared history and notes.

## Team Use Cases

| Team Type | Strategy |
|-----------|----------|
| **Engineering** | Shared channels for code review, debugging, and research. |
| **Marketing** | Collaboration on copy, campaigns, and content generation. |
| **Operations** | Unified AI assistant for workflow optimization and support. |
| **Product** | Brainstorming and research in organized, searchable channels. |
