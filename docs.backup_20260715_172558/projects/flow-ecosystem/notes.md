FlowChat includes a dedicated notes module for capturing, organizing, and linking knowledge directly to your AI conversations.

## What are Notes?

Notes are rich Markdown documents that let you:
- **Capture Insights**: Quickly save excerpts from AI chats into dedicated notes.
- **Organize Knowledge**: Create reusable resources, link them to specific conversations, and tag them for easy retrieval.
- **Search Everything**: Full-text search across all notes and chat history instantly.

## Features

### Rich Markdown Editor
- **Full Formatting**: Use bold, italics, headings, lists, and tables. 
- **Code Snippets**: Syntax-highlighted code blocks for developers. 
- **Attachments**: Add files and images directly to your notes.

### Smart Organization
- **Linking to Chats**: Connect notes to specific conversations for context. 
- **Pinning System**: Pin notes to the sidebar for quick access.

### Search & Retrieval
- **Full-Text Search**: Find keywords across your entire knowledge base. 
- **Filter by Tag**: Quickly surface notes based on their assigned tags. 
- **Chat Integration**: Access and reference your notes while in a conversation.

## Use Cases

| Use Case | Implementation Strategy |
|----------|-------------------------|
| **Prompt Library** | Store and refine your most successful prompts. |
| **Project Docs** | Link project-related chats to comprehensive documentation notes. |
| **Research Hub** | Gather AI-assisted research in one organized place. |
| **Team Wiki** | Share important insights and best practices across your organization. |
