**FlowSearch** is a privacy-respecting search engine built into the Flow Ecosystem, available at [search.flowchats.org](https://search.flowchats.org).

## Overview

FlowSearch combines a SearXNG-powered metasearch backend with a custom frontend, styled to match FlowChat, and adds an AI-generated answer summary above the results.

## Features

### AI Answer Box
- **Summary Above Results**: A Perplexity-style answer sits above your search results — concise, sourced, with inline citations.
- **Generated from Top Results**: The summary is built from the most relevant results for your query, not a separate AI call.

### Autocomplete
- **Suggestions as You Type**: Search suggestions appear in real time, helping you refine your query before hitting search.

### Flow Apps Result Cards
- **Integrated App Results**: Results relevant to FlowChat, FlowMovies, and FlowWeather surface as distinct cards rather than plain links.
- **Contextual Shortcuts**: Jump directly into a Flow app from a search result without navigating away.

### Privacy-First
- **No Query Tracking**: Built on SearXNG, FlowSearch doesn't log your searches or build an advertising profile.
- **No Account Required**: Search is available without signing in.
